03 Digital health · Synthetic-data prototype

ConsultLens

From scattered records to a consultation-ready patient journey. ConsultLens is a software service for doctors and care teams, with an initial focus on maternal and child health and antenatal follow-up.

Source-linked timelines, consultation briefings and an evidence review queue help clinicians inspect and correct information before approving notes.

Visit the ConsultLens prototype ↗

Not clinically validated. Uses synthetic data. No diagnosis or treatment advice.
